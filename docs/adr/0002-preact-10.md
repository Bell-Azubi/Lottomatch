# ADR-0002: Preact 10.29 instead of Preact 11

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

The brief prescribes Preact with `@preact/signals`. Preact 11.0 was released on 2026-09-30, about
five weeks before this build; 11.1.1 is the latest patch. The test and tooling ecosystem
(`@testing-library/preact` 3.2, the JSX typings consumed by TypeScript, signals integration) has
been exercised for years on Preact 10, whose last release is 10.29.8 (2026-08-01).

## Decision

Pin `preact@10.29.8` with `@preact/signals@2.11.4` (peer range `>= 10.25 || >= 11`).

## Consequences

- The app runs on the most battle-tested major line during live events.
- An upgrade to Preact 11 is a separate change once its ecosystem has settled; signals already
  supports it, so the upgrade is expected to touch types and tests only.
